How Tagvee Works


Getting started with Tagvee takes only a few minutes. Register your vehicle, add your emergency contact information, and generate your personal QR code.


Place the QR code on your vehicle. In any emergency or public assistance situation, anyone can scan it and instantly access safe communication options without revealing your private information.



Why Choose Tagvee?


Traditional vehicle identification methods often fail during emergencies. Tagvee provides a modern solution that enables quick communication between vehicle owners, emergency responders, and the public.


From parking issues to road accidents, every second matters. Our system helps bridge the communication gap when it matters most.
